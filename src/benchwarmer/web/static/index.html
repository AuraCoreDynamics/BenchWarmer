ℹ️ Understanding the Pareto Frontier (For Decision Makers)

What is Pareto Efficiency?
In machine learning deployment, every model represents a fundamental engineering trade-off: Capability (accuracy on benchmarks) vs. Hardware Cost (VRAM memory footprint in gigabytes).

The "No-Compromise" Frontier Curve:
The dashed cyan line connects the Pareto-optimal models. Each model on this curve is the best possible performer you can host for its memory size: you cannot get higher performance without upgrading your GPU memory.

Why models below the line are inefficient:
Any model sitting underneath or to the right of the line is strictly dominated. That means there is another model that delivers equal or greater intelligence while fitting into less VRAM (saving GPU cost, latency, and power).

How to use this practically:
1. Know your hardware budget: (e.g. 16 GB for RTX 4080 / Mac M-series, 24 GB for RTX 4090, 80 GB for an A100/H100 instance).
2. Look at the vertical line at your VRAM limit: The Pareto frontier model at or directly below that mark is the highest-intelligence model you can run!

Metric Guide:
• HF v2 Average: General balanced reasoning and language performance.
• MATH Hard: Pure math logic, symbolic proof, and problem-solving.
• IFEval: Instruction adherence, tool formatting, and JSON compliance.
• Arena Elo: Natural human conversation, style, and helpfulness.
